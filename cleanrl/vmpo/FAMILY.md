# V-MPO

## Objective

Build a controlled V-MPO research family for MuJoCo continuous control. The
family isolates V-MPO's policy-improvement mechanism from PPO and evaluates it
first on `HalfCheetah-v4` for 8 million steps with seed 1. This fixed-budget
benchmark is an adaptation, not a reproduction of the paper's Ant-v1 and
Walker2d-v1 deterministic-evaluation curves through 6 billion steps.

The algorithmic reference is Song et al., *V-MPO: On-Policy Maximum a
Posteriori Policy Optimization for Discrete and Continuous Control*
([arXiv:1909.12238](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12238)). The official RLax V-MPO
loss is the implementation reference for top-k weighting, temperature
optimization, and KL dual losses.

## Core training contract

Every active audited reference preserves these semantics unless its filename and
version entry identify one isolated ablation:

- trajectories are generated by a hard-frozen target policy;
- the target policy is copied from the online policy every 100 learner batches;
- OpenAI Gym unroll length is 39 and discount is 0.99;
- rollout boundaries do not reset environments: each environment contributes
  the next contiguous 39 transitions from its ongoing episode, and only actual
  termination or truncation starts a new episode;
- policy advantages use terminal-aware GAE with lambda `0.95`, online values,
  and bootstrap values from each transition's actual next observation;
- the critic regresses matching lambda returns, `GAE + rollout_value`;
- neither advantages nor returns are standardized, rank-transformed, or clipped;
- the highest 50% of advantages are selected globally across the fresh rollout;
- selected weights are a stopped-gradient global softmax of `advantage / eta`;
- temperature normalization and its sample count cover that same global set,
  matching RLax's `all_gather`/`AllSum` semantics under mapped devices;
- the policy loss is weighted negative log likelihood of observed behavior
  actions, not a PPO ratio or fitted target distribution;
- eta is solved to the convex E-step stationarity condition on every fresh
  rollout, targeting `KL(weights || selected_uniform)=epsilon_eta=0.01`;
- parametric policy KL penalties use learned positive Lagrange multipliers over
  the full batch;
- one Adam optimizer applies learning rate `3e-4`, the established PPO and
  IterThink PPO default, with betas `(0.9, 0.999)` and epsilon `1e-8` to network
  parameters and the parametric-KL dual variables, with no learning-rate
  annealing;
- exactly one full-batch joint network/dual update consumes each fresh rollout;
- no rollout-reuse epochs, sequential minibatches, PPO clipping, entropy
  regularization, importance weighting, policy early stopping, advantage
  clipping, or gradient clipping.

The Gym batch size is not reported. The family defaults to 64 parallel
trajectories, borrowed from the paper's humanoid experiments. This is an
explicit adaptation. RLax treats mapped devices as one logical E-step: it
gathers advantages to determine the global top-k threshold and all-reduces the
weight, selected-sample, and temperature-loss sums.

`SyncVectorEnv` advances every environment once per vector step. Equal
1,000-step HalfCheetah time limits made v6-v8 episode ages synchronous even
though reset seeds, states, and sampled actions differed; logs showed all 64
episodes ending together every 64,000 transitions. The active reference spends
one initial horizon on unrecorded stochastic target-policy warmup. Environment
ages are deterministically spaced at `horizon / num_envs`; a seed-local RNG
permutes the env-to-phase assignment. Scheduled resets establish those phases
without changing subsequent episode horizons. The 64,000 warmup interactions
count against the 8M transition budget.

The paper defines value evaluation and policy improvement as one joint loss and
shares most representation parameters between them. RLax supplies the V-MPO
loss operation but no critic optimizer schedule. Google Research's SEED RL
learner exposes a generic `epochs_per_step` control, defaulting to one, and
repeats the complete configured loss on every epoch. The v7 four-epoch
diagnostic confirmed that reuse improves critic fitting and dual motion, but it
did not equilibrate eta and is not part of the one-epoch reference.

## Beta carrier

The active carrier keeps IterThink v24's shared 64-wide iterative blocks and a
factorized, unimodal Beta action distribution. The entry state and three block
outputs are concatenated into the trunk's 256-wide output, avoiding v5's
256-to-64-to-256 bottleneck. Policy and value each then use a separate 256-unit
ReLU-squared MLP and linear readout.

```text
alpha = 1 + softplus(alpha_head(features))
beta  = 1 + softplus(beta_head(features))
```

Native samples in `(0, 1)` are affinely mapped to the environment action bounds.
The constant affine Jacobian can be omitted from policy optimization and KL
because it does not depend on policy parameters.

The paper's continuous actor is Gaussian and decouples mean and covariance KL.
The Beta carrier instead constrains two moment-coordinate changes separately:

- mean KL changes Beta mean while holding target concentration fixed;
- concentration KL changes Beta concentration while holding target mean fixed.

Both are analytic forward KLs from target to online policy. Their bounds default
to the geometric midpoints of the paper's Gym mean and covariance search ranges.
Full target-to-online Beta KL is logged as a diagnostic but is not a third
penalty.

## Candidate-free projected-target derivative

Version 19 is a controlled derivative, not a V-MPO or TPO reproduction. It
tests whether an explicit, self-terminating distribution target can retain
TPO's finite-target optimization without scoring multiple actions at the same
state. The online policy stays fixed while collecting one fresh rollout, so
the buffered Beta parameters are the exact behavior snapshot for the sole
learner update.

The signed GAE advantages are standardized over the full batch. For each native
action and factorized Beta dimension, v19 computes the score and Fisher
information of the mean coordinate at fixed behavior concentration:

```text
mean_score  = concentration * (
    log(action) - log(1 - action) - digamma(alpha) + digamma(beta)
)
mean_fisher = concentration^2 * (trigamma(alpha) + trigamma(beta))
direction   = standardized_advantage * mean_score / mean_fisher
```

A single batch-global scale is solved by bracketing and bisection so the batch
mean, action-summed forward KL from behavior to target is `0.007071`. Target
means retract along `direction` while preserving each behavior concentration
and remaining inside the carrier's `alpha,beta > 1` domain. The stopped target
is fitted with forward `KL(target || online)`, divided by the detached global
scale to recover the local projected policy-gradient magnitude. Its actor
gradient vanishes at the explicit target. The full fitting KL anchors
concentration to its behavior value, but mean/concentration coordinates can
still couple transiently during an optimizer step.

This first experiment deliberately drops the noisy single-action concentration
policy-gradient component. It has no candidate sampling, top-k filter, eta,
parametric-KL dual, or persistent target network. GAE, PopArt, observation
normalization, phase staggering, the model, and the one-update fresh-rollout
schedule remain inherited from v17.

V20 retains the same targets and target budget but makes fitting strictly
mean-coordinate. It rebuilds the online Beta at the detached behavior
concentration before evaluating `KL(target || online_mean)`. Gradients therefore
flow through the online mean only; reducing concentration cannot lower the actor
loss. This isolates the fitting-geometry repair from target construction.

V21 repairs the remaining output-coordinate leak. The actor emits one bounded
mean in `(0.05, 0.95)` and maps it to a smooth one-dimensional Beta carrier:

```text
concentration(mean) =
    0.1 * logsumexp([1 / mean, 1 / (1 - mean)] / 0.1)
    + 1.9 * log(2)
alpha = mean * concentration(mean)
beta  = (1 - mean) * concentration(mean)
```

This guarantees `alpha,beta > 1`, reproduces concentration `3.386` at mean
`0.5`, and leaves no independently trainable concentration direction. The
score and Fisher use the full derivative of both shapes along this carrier.
Line-search candidates and target fitting also use carrier distributions, not
fixed-concentration off-manifold Betas. Targets stop at the finitely reachable
interior means `0.0501` and `0.9499`.

## Value function

The paper's scalar squared value regression is retained with single-task PopArt.
PopArt updates running return mean and second moment at rate `1e-4`, clamps the
standard deviation to `[1e-2, 1e6]`, and rescales the final value layer so every
unnormalized prediction is invariant to statistic updates. Advantages and
bootstraps are always computed in unnormalized return units.

## Performance contract

The optimized carrier must preserve the mathematical contract above:

- FP32 master parameters, PopArt state, return arithmetic, distribution special
  functions, and KL calculations; V-MPO references also keep FP32 dual variables;
- BF16 autocast for neural-network matrix operations;
- batched expert parameters evaluated as two expert-batched GEMMs per block;
- static full-graph `torch.compile` with `reduce-overhead` CUDA graphs;
- compiled rollout, fused bootstrap-return, and global full-batch loss graphs;
- fused CUDA Adam and `zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`;
- no optimizer-path `.item()` calls or per-update diagnostic synchronization;
- one packed device-to-host training-metric transfer per logging interval;
- CUDA Graph outputs are consumed before the next explicit graph-step marker.

CPU environment stepping requires one action transfer from CUDA per environment
step. That synchronization is part of the Gym boundary, not the learner.

## Required diagnostics

Every active V-MPO reference version reports:

- `vmpo/eta`, `vmpo/e_step_kl`, and the exact eta stationarity residual
  `epsilon_eta - e_step_kl`;
- `vmpo/alpha_mean` plus the carrier-specific second multiplier
  (`alpha_concentration` for Beta or `alpha_covariance` for Gaussian), their
  aggregate motion across each rollout batch, and both KL-minus-bound residuals;
- `vmpo/mean_kl`, the carrier-specific `concentration_kl` or `covariance_kl`,
  and `full_beta_kl` or `full_gaussian_kl`;
- selected-weight ESS, ESS fraction, maximum weight, and perplexity fraction;
- target age in learner batches and environment transitions;
- raw advantage mean and standard deviation;
- unnormalized value RMSE and explained variance;
- Beta policy concentration/native variance, or Gaussian policy standard
  deviation mean, minimum, maximum, and condition ratio;
- PopArt mean and standard deviation;
- policy loss, value loss, temperature loss, SPS, and stochastic behavior-policy
  episodic return.

The v19 projected-target derivative instead reports behavior-to-target and
target-to-behavior KL, target-fit KL, KL-budget residual, target scale, mean
shift, carrier-boundary saturation, natural-score norm, post-update
behavior-to-online KL,
raw advantage moments, value diagnostics, Beta concentration/native variance,
PopArt statistics, policy/value losses, SPS, and stochastic behavior-policy
episodic return.

The behavior-policy episode return is the only policy-return measurement.
No separate policy-evaluation environment is run.
No family result is compared to the paper's continuous-control curves without
also matching task, environment version, evaluation protocol, run count, and
training scale.

## Ablation protocol

Each future hypothesis is a separate version derived from the current reference.
An ablation changes only its named dimension, retains the same benchmark command
and diagnostics, and never becomes an implicit default in another version.

- `V(s, a)` critic following the original MPO paper.

### fpo_v5 — ELBO-gap trust region with real Lagrangian duals

`ppo_continuous_action_iterthink_v24_fpo_v5_vmpo_elbo_trust_region_lagrangian_duals.py`

v4 (job 4552) reached only 75 @4M versus v30's 5127 and collapsed from 6M.
Two mechanisms, both in the trust region rather than the estimator:

- **Slow.** v30 crosses the 0.0071 mean-KL budget within the first 10
  batches of every window (promotion at age 9, 110 promotions by 4M). v4
  plateaued at 0.005 inside every window and promoted only at max age (18
  promotions by 4M). The plateau is an equilibrium between the ELBO-ratio
  objective, whose moment gradient carries E[t^2] = 1/3 of a log-likelihood's,
  and the moment penalty at alpha ≈ 1. The duals live in the policy Adam at lr
  3e-4 and never move (alpha_mean 1.00 ± 0.02 all run), so the trust region is
  a fixed penalty. The Gaussian is strong enough to reach the budget anyway;
  the flow is not.
- **Collapse.** The ratio objective rewards raising L_online without bound on
  pushed-down samples; the exp(-L) gradient decay is undone by Adam's
  normalization. Online CFM went 2 → 31 → 265 while the target's stayed 3–5,
  |pre-tanh| reached 40, and the K-draw moment KL saw nothing until the
  sampling paths broke (moment KL 143 with alpha_mean 1.02).

v5 changes: (1) an ELBO-gap constraint mean(L_online − L_target) ≤ 0.0071 on
the objective's own pairs; per sample the coefficient on ∇L_online,i is
(c_i r_i + alpha/B), which flips sign at r_i = alpha for pushed-down samples,
so the push-away stops at a ratio floor of alpha_elbo (PPO's lower clip as a
Lagrangian consequence). (2) All three duals take projected dual ascent in
relative units, alpha ← max(alpha + 0.1 (KL/eps − 1), 0), outside Adam.
(3) Promotion is checked every batch. (4) 32 CFM samples (16 antithetic
pairs), near free next to the coupled moment draws.

New scalars: `vmpo/alpha_elbo`, `vmpo/alpha_elbo_delta`.

Runs as MLQ job 4556 (`maxParallelRuns=1`). Compare against v30 at
500k/1M/2M/4M (−193 / −59 / 2007 / 5127) and watch promotion cadence
(`vmpo/target_promotions`, `vmpo/target_age_batches`) and the ratio floor
(`fpo/ratio_max`, `fpo/cfm_online` vs `fpo/cfm_target`).

fpo_v5 result (job 4556): final −293, never above random. The ELBO-gap dual
rose to ~10 within 500k steps and froze the policy (mean KL 3e-4, 7
promotions by 500k, all at max age). The estimator's own noise on the gap
keeps the constraint "violated" at any useful step size, so a real dual on it
cannot coexist with learning. Line closed; see eflow_v1 for the reasoning.

### eflow_v1 — exact-likelihood spline coupling flow on the v30 Beta head

`ppo_continuous_action_iterthink_v24_eflow_v1_vmpo_exact_spline_coupling_flow.py`

Why the FPO line failed as a class, not per bug: V-MPO takes one Adam step
per rollout batch, ~3200 steps per 8M run and ~10 per promotion, and the
M-step has to move a full trust-region budget in those ten steps. That needs
an exact score, a bounded weighted-MLE objective, zero self-fit drift, and a
head whose parameters map directly onto the distribution. The CFM ELBO ratio
has none of these in six dimensions (v3: drift; v4: unbounded push-away and
a 1/3-strength objective stuck under a fixed penalty; v5: noisy constraint
freezes the policy). FPO exists for intractable likelihoods. Ours is not.

Design (starts exactly at v30):
- a = T(u; s), u ~ Beta(alpha(s), beta(s)); T is 3 coupling layers of
  monotone rational-quadratic splines on [0, 1] (8 bins), knots from an MLP
  on the 256-d policy features and the untransformed half. Domain preserved,
  so the Beta edge mass and the action mapping are untouched. Conditioner
  output layers are zero-initialised: identity flow, zero log-det, at start.
- log pi(a|s) = log Beta(T^-1(a)) + log|det dT^-1/da|, exact both ways.
  M-step is v30's weighted NLL on it.
- Trust region: v30's decoupled Beta mean/concentration KLs are exact for the
  pushforward under a shared transform (KL invariance), so they keep their
  budgets. A third decoupled component, KL(T_t#B_t || T_o#B_t), is scored
  exactly on K=8 fresh target-base draws per state through the frozen
  target flow (only the sampling is MC; std of the mean ~8e-4 at budget), with
  v30's dual mechanics and the mean budget. Promotion fires on base mean KL or
  transform KL reaching the budget, checked on v30's log cadence.
- Exactness tests: `tests/test_eflow_v1_spline_coupling.py` (identity at
  init, log-det vs autograd Jacobian both directions, round trips, single
  spline monotone and exact inverse up to knot scale 3).

New scalars: `vmpo/transform_kl`, `vmpo/transform_kl_residual`,
`vmpo/full_flow_kl` (exact MC of the total KL), `vmpo/alpha_transform`,
`flow/policy_entropy` (exact, rollout samples), `flow/transform_log_abs_det`
(distance from identity), `flow/action_saturation`.

Runs as MLQ job 4558 (`maxParallelRuns=1`). Compare against v30 at
500k/1M/2M/4M/8M (−193 / −59 / 2007 / 5127 / 7429). The transform log-det
tells whether the flow ever leaves the identity; if it stays near zero the
run should reproduce v30 to within seed noise, which is the control.

eflow_v1 result (job 4558): 101 @1M, 842 @2M, 3378 @4M, 4971 final
(v30: −59 / 2007 / 5127 / 7548). Same 13k SPS as v30. The transform did all
the moving: transform KL per window 0.017–0.034 against its 0.0071 budget,
base mean KL 0.0004–0.007 (v30: 0.015), promotions every 9 batches from the
first window (17 by 500k vs v30's 1). Spline knots are far more sensitive
per Adam step than the Beta head and the transform dual sat at 1.0, so the
policy overshot the E-step target 3–5× per window and partly fit the batch's
top-half selection: faster start, lower plateau, saturation 0.5% → 6.5%.

### eflow_v4 — coordinate-orthogonal recurrent flow with hard KL feasibility

`ppo_continuous_action_iterthink_v24_eflow_v4_vmpo_projected_recurrent_flow.py`

The rollout now stores the target Beta draw. The carrier M-step fits that
latent action directly, while a parameter-disjoint flow M-step scores the
native action under the frozen target Beta. This restores v30's direct carrier
gradient and leaves the spline to learn residual joint shape. The policy and
critic condition on the previous native action, reset to box center at episode
boundaries, so the exact conditional flow can represent temporal as well as
cross-joint dependence.

The transform dual is removed. After every Adam proposal, exact-density KL on
the same eight target-base probes either accepts the flow/context update,
geometrically backtracks it, or restores a guaranteed-feasible origin. A
feasible pre-step policy uses itself as the origin; a batch whose pre-step
estimate is already outside the radius uses the frozen target transform. The
target promotion cadence returns to v30's mean-KL/max-age rule.

benchmark.

eflow_v4 result (job 4600): −188 @500k, 50 @1M, 2372 @2M, 4664 @4M,
6307 @6M, 7328 @7.5M, and 7589 final-20 at 7.776M, versus v30's
−189 / −62 / 1989 / 5138 / 6580 / 7546 and 7790 final-20. The run then

controller, but target promotion still made each accumulated warp the next

### qteacher_v1 — replay twin-Q proposal teacher with exact distillation

`ppo_continuous_action_iterthink_v24_qteacher_v1_replay_twinq_distill.py`

The binding limitation is counterfactual action information, not density
capacity. v30 and every eflow version observe one executed action per state;
an expressive flow receives the same scalar trajectory weight as the Beta
head. The prior fresh-rollout candidate-Q experiment v99 confirmed that a
single on-policy Q fit is insufficient: it reached only 282 @500k and 1063
@1M versus its Beta parent's 1660 / 3395. In contrast, the independent HOPSD
v40 replay twin-Q teacher reached 4039 @500k and 6256 @1M. That cross-chassis
result is not a v30 comparison, but it identifies replayed Q-ascent followed
by pure distillation as the strongest existing action-credit mechanism.

qteacher_v1 returns the deployed policy to v30's identifiable, observation-only
Beta actor and keeps its Dreamer moment-matched value critic, exact E-step,
decoupled actor KL constraints, and target promotion. A one-million-transition
raw-observation replay ring trains LayerNorm twin Q critics with three-step
raw-reward targets, Polyak targets, UTD approximately 8, and one fresh
raw-reward TD(lambda) hardening pass per rollout. Replay rows retain their
environment identity: actor/teacher queries use that environment's current
normalization while Q uses stable pooled normalization.

A private Beta teacher is the sole policy-improvement engine. It retains
v30's exact trajectory-weighted NLL, then ascends pessimistic min-Q through
reparameterized actions on an equal mix of fresh and replay states. A
fixed-radius Q-frontier probe normalizes the action gradient. Analytic
`KL(teacher || student)` and projected dual ascent bound the teacher's
privilege at 0.6 nats. The deployed actor takes one exact forward-KL
distillation step toward the stopped teacher, under v30's existing
target-to-online mean/concentration constraints. Q gradients never enter the
deployed actor or shared value trunk.

Runs as MLQ job 4631 (`maxParallelRuns=1`, no time limit, no autocull) on the
8M seed-1 HalfCheetah-v4 benchmark. Mechanism diagnostics include fresh
Q explained variance, replay Bellman RMSE, twin gap, fixed-radius frontier,
teacher min-Q gain over behavior, teacher-to-student KL/dual motion, replay
size, and both teacher/student concentration and variance.
