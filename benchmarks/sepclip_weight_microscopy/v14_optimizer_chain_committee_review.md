# V14 optimizer chain: independent source committee review

Read-only source/JSON review; no checkpoints/models loaded, numerical execution, jobs or trainer changes. Frozen v14 source SHA256: 27a3e7a84e2c5d0ab1679f84cc4ddb8dad5eb1b6e2bc5f66850ad93ccf1012b0. The observed additive-Anvil underperformance motivates a mechanism audit, but cannot identify a unique architectural or optimizer cause. The source supports the following exact chain and a narrowly scoped follow-up if mechanism attribution is required.

The additive actor uses one signed projection and two identity residual blocks. Seven matrices receive the selected matrix optimizer: projection64x17, four gate/up43x64 matrices and two down64x43 matrices. Same-shape stacking therefore produces three banks, with state owned by the first serialized parameter of each bank rather than independent state entries for all seven parameters. Projection and down banks are tall; gate/up banks are wide. This differs from the original actor's first gate/up43x17 matrices, including lane axis and aspect scaling, and is part of the compound architectural change.

The complete actor gradient is clipped once over body matrices, projection/block biases, mixing vectors, head and readout gains. Thus head gradients affect the common body clipping multiplier and body gradients affect the head multiplier, even though body matrices subsequently use Anvil/NorMuon and remaining parameters use fused Adam. Critic gradients are clipped separately. The stored gradient_after_clip is the clipped loss covector, including any configured entropy term; policy-only ascent in the layer audit has a separate explicit meaning. No unclipped per-parameter covector or per-step clipping multiplier is stored in the ten-entry trace.

For each stacked matrix bank let G be that recorded clipped covector. Anvil updates fast rail V0 toward G with scheduled decay b_fast and slow rail V1 with its source clock. Under rollout clock, begin_rollout(10) sets pending=True and decay=.98^10; only the first epoch updates V1 toward its first gradient, with coefficient1-.98^10. Later epochs use slow_beta=1 and keep V1 unchanged. This is not an average of ten epoch gradients. The scheduled fast/slow blend B = w_fast*V0+(1-w_fast)*V1 enters lookahead N=(1-m)*G+m*B. The source rail schedule uses the PRE-increment group step, rescaled to its1194-step reference phase; group steps is incremented before the bank kernels run.

NorMuon updates only V0=(.85*V0+.15*G) for the intended run. V1 is allocated but unused and should remain identically zero from initialization. Its lookahead N=.15*G+.85*V0 uses the freshly updated rail, not the old momentum. NorMuon beta2=.95; additive Anvil uses the actual default beta2=.9 because its submitted command does not override it. Both .005 commands anneal their LR; the dispatch label actor_optimizer=anvil2 selects matrix-plus-Adam, while matrix_method selects the actual method. Comparing these runs therefore changes moments, cascade and lane statistics together, rather than isolating one preconditioner feature.

N then passes through a method-specific cascade. BF16 Anvil casts N, forms its unnormalized BF16 Gram, normalizes from the FP32 Gram trace with1.05 and epsilon1e-6, reuses that normalized Gram for the first of six maps, and uses fused addmm/addbmm rounding. NorMuon uses the five Polar Express maps and norm normalization with1.02 and epsilon1e-6. Identical precision=bf16 labels do not mean identical rounding or polynomial maps.

Let C be the resulting FP32 cascade direction. The lane second moment updates S=beta2*S+(1-beta2)*mean_lane(C²). For tall/square matrices the reduction is over columns, retaining row lanes; for wide matrices it reduces rows, retaining column lanes. It measures cascade-direction power, not raw-gradient variance. A=C/sqrt(max(S,1e-10)); E=A*norm(C)/max(norm(A),1e-10). Thus lane adaptation is followed by restoration of cascade Frobenius norm, before any final quantization. BF16 Anvil then rounds E to BF16 and back to FP32; NorMuon does not add this final requantization. Master weights update W -= annealed_lr*sqrt(max(1,rows/columns))*E. Actual FP32 subtraction, not the ideal product alone, determines the stored ambient delta.

Auxiliary fused Adam independently updates biases, sigmoid-mix vectors, head weights and readout gain, plus all critic parameters. Its exp_avg, exp_avg_sq and step history determine these actual proposals. The actor head rows and original critic body/head axes are then normalized. Additive body matrices remain free: for them recorded ambient and retracted delta should agree exactly absent a recording defect; gradient normal/tangent splitting must not be imposed on them. Adam head moments are not sphere-transported by this normalization. Scale compensation between free body, sigmoid mixing and head gain is a source-supported hypothesis, not yet measured as a cause.

Snapshot timing matters. LR annealing occurs before the captured rollout. The saved pre-update snapshot includes model, actual Args, matrix/Adam states and the complete rollout batch. It is saved BEFORE begin_rollout(10). Matrix group fields include method, precision, momentum, beta2, slow_clock, total_steps, steps, rollout_pending, rollout_decay and rollouts, plus live lr/scalars tensors. The saved scalars may describe the preceding step: actual step overwrites them and the strict loader rebuilds them. Merely reading saved scalar values would misidentify the next update. Bank state velocity has shape[2,bank_size,rows,columns], second has the matching retained lane layout. Adam serialization separately maps parameter IDs to moments/step. Parameter IDs must be bound through the exact actual factory/group order, not sorted names or shape alone.

The ten trace entries store clipped gradients, ambient deltas and post-normalization deltas for EVERY parameter, with epoch/update indices; the exact final full model is saved. This directly identifies actual step sizes, signed gradient/update pairings, free versus constrained normalization corrections and reconstructed weight motion at each epoch. The layer audit adds source-dispatched activations, old-batch policy credit, joint Fisher cross terms and finite shape KL after its numerical gates. The trace alone does NOT store updated rails, lane second moments, lookahead/cascade outputs, adaptation factors, clipping multipliers, unclipped gradients or per-epoch independent checkpoints. Final TensorBoard bank-mean gradient/momentum cosine and epsilon fraction describe only the last step and average over bank members; they cannot establish an individual-layer ten-epoch lag history.

A narrowly targeted NEW moment forensic diagnostic is necessary before asserting a particular momentum/cascade/lane mechanism, but is not necessary just to report the benchmark regression. Existing captures should suffice; no new training or physical rollout is required. Use the frozen strict replay loader and optional exact-module warm loader, private old weights/optimizer state, call source begin_rollout(10), then feed ONLY the ten recorded clipped gradients to the actual source optimizer/Adam steps and normalization. First require every replayed ambient/post-normalization delta and exact final model to match the saved trace within explicit FP32 numerical contracts. This is reconstruction of the recorded update sequence, not hypothetical candidate proposals. A failure must withhold moment interpretation, not refit or silently alter saved gradients.

After that parity gate, record each bank member's before/after rails, source schedule scalars, blend/lookahead norms and signed alignment, exact source cascade norm/singular spectrum, lane S range and clamp-active fraction, adaptation relative to C, aspect-scaled proposal and actual FP32 displacement. Preserve both Adam head/mix/gain moment metadata and the complete actor clipping context. Mark internal moment stages as source-reconstructed rather than independently recorded; endpoint/trace agreement is their acceptance evidence. Compare functional and stored-PPO credit of the ACTUAL motion through the existing layer audit, rather than inventing an alternative optimizer motion. Source-reconstructed moment alignment is not physical PG or a causal return decomposition.

The falsifiable question is whether the recorded gradient direction is already weak on the saved surrogate, whether actual moment/cascade/lane transformation rotates or scales it into a different local functional motion, or whether substantial parameter motion is cancelled by joint functional cross terms and mix/head compensation. Layer outputs can establish these local distinctions after derivative/Fisher checks; a replay-approved moment audit can locate the transformation stage. None of these alone explains20k performance or isolates a single cause in the additive package: policies, visited states, critic errors, body constraints, depth/parameterization and optimizer trajectories differ. Reliable physical-field pairings and controlled intervention remain separate evidence gates.
