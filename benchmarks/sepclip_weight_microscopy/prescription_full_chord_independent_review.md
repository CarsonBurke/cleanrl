Final amendments add output-storage telemetry, adjacent response normalization, an omitted-body derivative witness and an independent ULP denominator fixture.

The numerical kernel requires complete finite FP32 CUDA actor banks/directions and recorded observations, with no implicit actor buffers. Actual analysis uses the separately reviewed strict v14/v15 replay loader, all saved batch observations, the raw saved first retracted delta and the actual recorded endpoint minus old state. First-model reconstruction limits and missing independently recorded old logits remain in replay metadata. No legacy unit-row tangent projection or retraction is applied to finite-difference neighbors, including v14 free-body/input-projection/residual parameters.

JVP is evaluated at the old bank along the declared full parameter chord. A separate compiled primal evaluates old+h*direction and old-h*direction; its FP32 outputs are cloned and promoted before centered subtraction/(2h). The independently compiled old primal must agree with the primal returned by the JVP graph. This supplies distinct derivative and finite-difference paths without treating endpoint finite changes as an exact local derivative. Both latent logits and alpha/beta responses are reported, and the smooth AD shape convention is explicitly distinct from differentiation of FP32 output rounding.

Every requested decreasing positive h is retained. The script checks finite normal FP32 scalar range and withholds nonfinite neighboring parameters or outputs. It reports effective rounded parameter direction, requested-direction error, center drift and vanished nonzero coordinates. Complete loss of representability is withheld; partial loss is visible and requires interpretation. Adjacent response differences are reported both absolutely and relative to JVP norm, with each chord's lost-coordinate fraction. No post-hoc best-h selection or automated derivative pass is claimed. Null JVP/finite responses preserve undefined relative error/cosine rather than invent numerical floors.

For each response, output-storage telemetry uses the actual FP32 plus/minus outputs' toward-zero ULP spacings, sums those spacings and divides by 2h, then reports L2/max and relative JVP norm. At zero it uses the smallest positive FP32 spacing. The independent fixture at output1,h=.5 correctly expects per-element2^-23 and L2 twice that for four elements. This is a storage-spacing heuristic, deliberately not a rigorous rounding-error bound: toward-zero binade spacing and compiled internal arithmetic can differ, and accumulated primal roundoff is excluded. Low heuristic floor alone cannot certify correctness; a high floor warns that a finite difference cannot resolve the derivative at that scale.

The tests detect several concrete failures without executing actual trials: an affine bias chord has an independent exact derivative; representational loss and Beta shape-floor plateaus are separated; actual v14 original/additive and v15 nonlinear all-parameter directions require centered-primal agreement across all three tested scales. A deliberate actual additive-body detach preserves forward values but erases body AD. With a body-only input-projection radial chord it produces null JVP and nonzero centered primal/error at every scale, so the discrepancy is exposed even if a head-heavy aggregate direction could hide omitted body credit. Additional fixtures separately cover nonfinite parameter neighbors, finite parameters with overflowing exponential outputs, invalid step metadata and null-response reporting. These are source-read contracts, not passing results.

Actual-capture acceptance must remain separate: require matching immutable execution/source/checkpoint/trace provenance and independently executed contracts; inspect at least two adjacent scales with usefully represented requested chord, stable nonnull latent finite differences, suitably small relative error and aligned cosine, plus non-dominating output-storage heuristic. Declare numerical acceptance tolerances before using actual measurements for derivative claims. Retain all scales and report unresolved cases rather than selecting a favorable h. The default1,.5,.25,.125,.0625 range may be insufficient when late small chords are dominated by FP32 rounding or large endpoint chords remain nonlinear. Shape-floor finite differences cannot validate nonzero smooth shape AD on a rounding plateau; latent validation and shape-response qualification are separate.

Agreement is restricted to these saved observations, chord and numerical convention. It does not certify future visited states, physical PG, retained-range efficiency, warm optimizer continuation, finite returns, global architecture capacity or benchmark improvements. The deliberately corrupted-body test remains a measured mismatch rather than a source-level automatic rejection because this audit produces telemetry and makes no automatic certification claim.
