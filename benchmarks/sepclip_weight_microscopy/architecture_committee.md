## SiTU: exact symmetry and the normalization caveat

Write c(g)=4*tanh(g/4), d(u)=25*tanh(u/25), and f(g,u)=c(g)*sigmoid(g)*d(u). Under simultaneous sign reversal, its exact even and odd parts are:

- E(g,u)=0.5*c(g)*d(u).
- O(g,u)=E(g,u)*tanh(g/2).
- f=E+O; f(-g,-u)=E-O.

Near zero, E≈0.5*g*u and O≈0.25*g²*u. Thus the raw branch has a zero first derivative at the origin and leading quadratic, sign-insensitive features. This statement does NOT establish a vanishing Jacobian of the normalized actor. For x=r*v away from zero branch directions, B(x)=r²*B2(v)+r³*B3(v)+..., so normalize(B(x))=normalize(B2(v))+O(r). Angular input derivatives can scale as 1/r; signed/radial information, rather than all gradient magnitude, is what is suppressed near this limit. F.normalize's epsilon supplies a separate extremely small regime. Report actual visited-state norms, gate distributions, odd/even feature energy and input JVPs before interpreting this asymptotic argument as a control bottleneck.

Actual derivatives are q'(g)=sech²(g/4)*sigmoid(g)+c(g)*sigmoid(g)*(1-sigmoid(g)), with q=c*sigmoid, and d'(u)=sech²(u/25). A gate can transmit or attenuate a physical velocity direction depending on its current g, up value, downstream projection and unit-sphere tangent projection. The Jacobian of normalize(y) is (I-h*h^T)/||y||. The residual unit sum introduces another such projector; if h and branch almost oppose, its small norm can amplify gradients. Inspect their cosine and denominator quantiles. A branch activation magnitude alone is insufficient.

The first branch has 43 gated channels for 17 inputs. This is not an immediate rank ceiling on the 17-dimensional state tangent; blindly widening does not answer the signed-coordinate problem.

## Head initialization: definite cancellation, uncertain learning consequence

The actor head is orthogonally initialized with std=.01, then each row is normalized to unit length, while readout gains start at one. Because output dimension 12 is below hidden width 64, the orthogonal head row norm is .01 before retraction; row normalization removes that intended factor exactly up to arithmetic. Under an isotropic unit feature, 8*head*feature has per-output variance near one, rather than .0001. Actual initial-logit RMS must still be measured because features need not be isotropic.

This is a real initialization semantic discrepancy. It does not prove the late return ceiling. A gain=.01 correction also changes gradient conditioning. Hidden raw PG gradients scale with gain, whereas polar or adaptive normalization can remove much of that scalar scaling. Functional steps still include the gain in the logit Jacobian. Gain derivatives themselves need not be small, and Adam at .0024 can move gain rapidly over early updates. Therefore a small-gain run needs early raw/tangent/functional step and gain trajectories, not an assertion that it merely restores standard PPO's benign small-head initialization. Keep it an isolated intervention if tested.

## Beta geometry: capacity, conditioning and physical questions

For each native action y in (0,1), k=alpha+beta and m=alpha/k. The physical mean is a=2*m-1. With alpha,beta=1+softplus(z), both shapes exceed one. This implies |a|<1-2/k: at k=16–20 the mean is bounded within approximately .875–.9; a mean .98 requires k>100. This is conditional, not an ultimate network bound: readout gains and biases are learned. The distribution mode can approach the torque bounds independently of this mean limit.

The exact logit-score components are sigmoid(z_alpha)*(log(y)-digamma(alpha)+digamma(k)) and sigmoid(z_beta)*(log(1-y)-digamma(beta)+digamma(k)). Derivatives dm/dz_alpha=beta*sigmoid(z_alpha)/k² and dm/dz_beta=-alpha*sigmoid(z_beta)/k², while dk/dz equals sigmoid(z) for both heads. Consequently changing either shape logit jointly changes mean and concentration; high concentration makes a given mean movement require larger logit movement. At very negative shape logits the softplus derivative attenuates score transmission. This is a conditioning hypothesis, not a statement that bang-bang torque is optimal.

Measure all of: sampled/mean/mode episode return, action second moment, proximity to bounds, velocity, contact/gait statistics and control cost. If mean is much better than sampling, exploration noise has a measured closed-loop cost. If mode is much better than mean, shape geometry is worth testing. If all are similarly suboptimal and the action oracle requires representable signed corrections, the problem is gait or learned direction rather than action-range capacity alone. Changed mean/concentration parameterization can preserve alpha,beta>1 only by retaining a coupled feasible mean region; claiming fully independent unrestricted mean and concentration with those constraints is mathematically inconsistent.

## Critic: what does and does not bias PG

Actor and critic have independent trunks and separate .5 norm clipping. A large critic loss cannot directly steal actor gradient budget here. Current low gradient norms also reject a clipping-starvation explanation. Critic gain or explained variance against its own bootstrapped returns does not establish a correct policy gradient.

Let e_t=V_est(s_t)-V_ref(s_t), and compare GAE computed from the same trajectory/rewards with estimated versus reference values. For an uninterrupted H-step segment with q=gamma*lambda:

A_est-A_ref = -e_t + gamma*(1-lambda)*sum[j=1..H-1](q^(j-1)*e_(t+j)) + gamma*q^(H-1)*e_(t+H).

Termination/truncation/reset boundaries require their actual masks and distinct bootstrap values. Flattened advantages/returns do not reconstruct these quantities. At gamma=.99, lambda=.95, the infinite future-error kernel mass is .99*.05/(1-.9405)=.83193; substantial critic error can propagate forward even when the instantaneous baseline contribution cancels. Reference-value GAE is still a noisy finite-trajectory estimate, not the true advantage or unbiased Monte Carlo oracle.

For the expected fresh-policy *unclipped* score gradient, the state-only -e_t baseline contributes zero because E[score|s]=0. Finite samples leave a measurable residual. Crucially this cancellation does not generally hold in later clipped PPO epochs. The active gradient is -A*r*score*m, where m is zero for (A>=0 and r>1.2) or (A<0 and r<.8), and otherwise one. A state-only baseline shift can change A's sign and therefore the action-selective clipping mask. The critic audit must distinguish fresh-score future-error bias, baseline sampling noise, and clipped-mask sensitivity. Merely multiplying a global critic MSE by a gradient norm conflates them.

Reward normalization introduces row-specific, time-varying positive scales and clipping. The learner's gamma=.99 normalized discounted reward objective differs from the reported raw undiscounted episode sum. Evaluate raw and frozen-normalized reward action oracles separately; disagreement could reflect objective mismatch rather than critic estimator failure. Normalizer row choice, resets and adaptation must be explicit and identical across paired forks.

## Recommended decisive test, conditional on microscopy

If signed physical-coordinate JVPs are deficient on actual visited states and the physical oracle asks for those directions, the most interpretable architecture intervention is a fixed positive gate offset in the actor FIRST branch only: replace q(Gx) by q(Gx+c), with one declared c (e.g. 1), leaving Ux, down projection, second stage, critic, distribution, optimizer settings and all training semantics unchanged. At x=0 the raw first derivative becomes q(c)*D*U, providing a linear signed transport path. This changes no parameter count and does not widen the critic. A zero-offset implementation must reproduce v7, including host-mirror parity. Implement in a distinct frozen v12+ single file and compare offset versus zero with both Adam and Anvil: a 2x2 architecture/optimizer design, not four tuned unrelated methods. It tests whether the same architecture bottleneck caps both optimizers.

The offset is a proposal, not yet selected on evidence. Global x versus -x flips all physical coordinates and often leaves the visited manifold, so parity alone does not justify it. Require local signed-velocity perturbations, actual feature changes and score/Fisher-weighted reachability of the physical oracle. If those are healthy, reject this intervention and choose the measured bottleneck instead. Do not bundle head-gain repair, new action distribution, critic changes or tangent transport into the same first causal test.

For an action-noise finding instead, test an explicit mean/concentration parameterization with labeled feasibility and sampling differences, matched Adam/Anvil controls and exact action protocols. For a transport finding, use tangent moment transport only after demonstrating lost functional PG alignment rather than high radial parameter norm alone.

## Round 4: prepared anchored chart candidate, before causal selection

Read-only adversarial review of root's prepared `ppo_continuous_action_sepclip_anchored_precision_chart_v13.py` and its contracts. No experiment selection or measured improvement is established by this review.

Let q be Beta mode in native coordinates, r=kappa-2 the excess concentration and c=(mode_logit, log(r)). The chart defines alpha=1+r*q and beta=1+r*(1-q). It preserves the alpha,beta>1 family and initializes at the existing policy mathematically. Mode and concentration are independently named coordinates, but their Beta-Fisher cross term is generally nonzero:

    F_mode,logr = r²*q*(1-q)*[q*trigamma(1+r*q) - (1-q)*trigamma(1+r*(1-q))].

At fixed q, increasing concentration also moves the physical mean a=r/(r+2)*(2*q-1), with da/dlogr=2*r*(2*q-1)/(r+2)². Therefore this is not an exact orthogonal split of physical action mean and precision. Quantify the full 2x2 Fisher and achieved mean/mode/concentration changes. A claim of orthogonal physical-PG channels would be false.

The anchored architecture has a second distinction. Write z'=Decode(Encode(z_anchor(h))+z_train(h)-z_anchor(h)). Its hidden Jacobian is J_Decode*[J_train+(J_Encode-I)*J_anchor]. At initialization J_train=J_anchor and J_Decode*J_Encode=I, preserving the original hidden sensitivity. After head motion, the fixed initial readout contributes a nonlinear functional basis on the CURRENT learned features. The method is an anchored chart architecture, not solely a conventional coordinate change of the same linear-head function class. Keeping trainable parameter count and initial hidden Jacobian equal does not remove this later basis difference. The alpha_beta control takes the original trainable logits directly and appears to implement the correct exact control for arbitrary weights.

Potential numerical issues require measurement before causal training. Exponentiating log excess concentration can create very high Beta shapes before floating-point overflow. FP32 lgamma/digamma subtraction in likelihood, entropy, score and KL can then lose accuracy even with finite outputs. At more extreme positive log precision, the chart overflows; at very negative precision or extreme mode logits, excess shapes can underflow to zero, inverse-softplus produces -inf, and its backward can form nonfinite intermediates. Existing moderate FP64 shape tests do not establish the actual FP32 likelihood or derivative operating range. A concentration cap would change the experiment rather than diagnose arithmetic.

The new owned `scripts/audit_sepclip_chart_numerics.py` and focused contracts prepare a compiled CUDA audit of concentrations 3,10,30,100,1e3,1e4,1e6 across modes .01,.1,.5,.9,.99. FP64 likelihood/entropy/score/exact-KL references cast the SAME actual FP32 shapes and actions; this separates arithmetic error from distribution changes. Softplus-logit scores and chart-coordinate scores are both compared. Tiny modal/precision chart changes expose cancellation in exact KL. The real 16-env, 6-action host sampler is checked with copied permanent buffers, theoretical moments/standard errors and clipping probabilities. An additional actual candidate decoder range reports nonfinite forward/backward values as null across log excess concentration -120 through 100 and mode logits up to ±100, without clamping. Root owns queued execution and any precision decision; no CUDA results have yet been produced by this committee member.

Before attributing a future return gain to conditioning, demand: independently positive physical-oracle pairing for its realized mean/concentration motion at matched Fisher cost; actual whole-policy budget including hidden/head/gain cross terms; a correctly loaded chart checkpoint in diagnostics; and a fresh architecture-matched Adam/Anvil contrast. A chart that merely increases concentration, creates a larger functional step, or changes the fixed-anchor feature basis does not by itself prove that the prior optimizer was missing a globally optimal gait.
