Independent source/protocol review only; no new measurement, model execution, test or submission. 1. Accept an artifact only after its exact executed sources, contracts, snapshot/trace/input hashes, state order and complete cases agree. DenseH512 must actually contain four snapshots,32 states,16 independent first-action draws, two complete noise replicas, gamma.99 and explicit first_action_only=true. Require saved physical restore/first-transition errors<=1e-10, historical GAE/bootstrap replay<=2e-5 and applied-reward replay<=1e-6, using the source's precise comparison semantics. Require finite preclip policy/draw/reward/next observation and accumulation, no continuation boundaries, correct means of recorded replicas, and analytic-score replay consistent with its executed test contract. The prose protocol gives no standalone numerical threshold for that last telemetry: do not invent one after seeing the result. Same-source test acceptance and its relative error should both be recorded.

2. A fixed signed H512 scalar is mean_states dot(g_replica, motion), where motion is fixed by training history before these oracle draws. Declare ambient chord JVP, finite logit difference or tangent intervention explicitly. For an actual derivative claim use the matched independent nonlinear JVP contracts, not the finite-logit pairing. Check raw finite, frozen-normalized finite and normalized-plus-old-critic-tail on the same motion and replica ordering. Preserve the paired tail increment. The old-tail result is an estimated augmented objective, not true infinite credit; dense reward rounding and online normalizer differences remain material scope limits.

3. For two scalar replicas x1,x2, ordinary conditional95% interval half-width=6.353*abs(x1-x2). Use the previously proposed screening criterion: interval excludes zero AND half-width<=.5*abs(mean). This is a precision screen, not a familywise significance certificate or bound on score-law bias. AtR2 it requires abs(mean)>=12.706*abs(x1-x2). A failed screen means unresolved scalar direction, not zero effect. Same-sign replicas alone do not pass. Conditional intervals omit state-population and training-seed uncertainty and rely on a fragile approximate replica distribution.

4. Family multiplicity is currently an unresolved registration gap. Neither reviewed protocol fixes a primary local direction/objective or an alpha budget for the many states, layers, horizons, snapshots, optimizer motions and trial outcomes. Keep every unadjusted interval descriptive unless its claim family was declared before inspecting those values. A possible restricted H512 family of four snapshots x two fixed update motions x three objectives already has24 contrasts; all five horizons would make120. Adding finite/tangent alternatives, layers, feedback interactions or modes expands that family. A conservative familywise interval can use t_{R-1,1-.05/(2M)} for declaredM; no independence across contrasts is needed for the Bonferroni guarantee, but the marginal t approximation still needs justification. WithR2 the critical value is cot(pi*.05/(2M)), much larger than12.706. Do not quietly reuse the pointwise50%-width screen as if it addressed multiplicity. If no defensible family was registered, report signed exploratory estimates and all failures without selective significance language.

5. Inspect full-field signed cross-energy/noise and retained-range numerical stability separately. AtR2 there is one cross-energy pair. Positive cross-energy alone does not establish resolved field; negative cross-energy remains a signed noisy estimate. A stable fixed scalar can be accepted locally while fitting/efficiency remains withheld. Pair entries for largerR share replicas; reciprocal fit/test directions are dependent; choosing among held-replica gains consumes that holdout. Preserve actual rounded-shape KL versus smooth-FP64 Fisher distinction, all SVD thresholds and discarded motion. No robust full-field acceptance threshold or field-energy confidence procedure is registered; absent a separately justified procedure, label fitted ratios descriptive and do not claim near-optimality or chart preference.

6. Closed-loop first/end effects are paired FINITE outcomes, not predictions from the dense first-action oracle. Require exact actualold/first/end banks, source-specific actor forwards at all visited observations, per-state frozen normalization, full integration/recorded-action replay<=1e-10, no resets, finite traces, source reward rounding and source gamma powers. Old fixed-critic tails must be separate from zero-tail returns. The reconstructed first bank is old+saved FP32delta; original first weights are not independently saved. Endpoint is exact saved final model. Verify actual trace epoch/update count before calling it a ten-epoch effect; endpoint-minus-old measures the aggregate endpoint intervention, not a sum of sequential epoch derivatives.

7. Compute each full gain, first-action-only gain, later-only gain and feedback interaction WITHIN the same replica before summarizing across independent complete replicas. The interaction is full-first-later+old, including every covariance induced by common quantiles. The difference between first and endpoint gains must also be paired within replica; independent-arm or independent-horizon error propagation would be wrong. Four sampled replicas use ordinary pointwise t3 critical3.182; their precision remains conditional on these selected states. Mean/mode forks have a deterministic paired difference under this state/action convention and no continuation-MC interval; zero reported MCSE is not zero state or seed uncertainty. Their sampling family must be counted if making simultaneous directional claims.

8. A stronger claim needs a numerical threshold registered for a practically useful return gain. The reviewed protocols contain no minimal meaningful closed-loop effect, no horizon-convergence criterion and no independent physical score-gradient bias bound. Report gain in raw return, normalized return, velocity/control cost and its interval rather than manufacture these thresholds. If dense first-action prediction and full closed-loop gain disagree, first compare the correct t0-only arm, objective, terminal tail and action/normalizer law. Full-policy occupancy feedback, finite update curvature and sampling noise are alternatives; disagreement cannot be assigned to weights, critic bias or optimizer geometry by sign alone. A passing nonlinear JVP does not close the quantile truncation/clipping-atom/FP32 law gap.

If the legacy closed-loop artifact also contains an all-time old-trajectory directional score estimate, verify its sum over every visited time with gamma^t weighting and its separate terminal term gamma^H*sum_t(score_t)*V_old(endpoint). It must not be replaced by H times dense t0 credit. Compare finite gain minus directional prediction within replica, retaining their covariance. Same-trajectory GAE-minus-tail-augmented-MC is an estimator difference for that finite augmented objective, not an independently established critic-bias measurement.

Disposition: verified numerical artifacts are measurements; precision-qualified fixed scalars are conditional local estimates under the diagnostic score/sampling law; exact paired finite effects are conditional simulator interventions. A fresh benchmark improvement supports the tested whole actor/optimizer package atseed1 under matched execution. It does not isolate signed anchoring, normalization, retained amplitude, transport history or concentration, prove the microscopic cause, or establish seed-general superiority. Missing gates stay explicitly unresolved while user-authorized exploratory trials proceed.
