The precision repair is substantive. Across full-step, component and counterfactual KL fields, the previous report had 36 negative values among 168; the new report has none, with minimum 3.121920e-12. At v4 half 8M, the old full-step KL was -8.909410e-7 and is now +1.954665e-7; 27 of that snapshot's 28 old KL values were negative. At rollout half 8M the old full KL 1.191277e-6 overstated the repaired value by about 5.30 times. Every shared non-KL field is exactly unchanged between old and new JSON. The source preserves actual rounded FP32 alpha/beta and promotes only Beta KL special-function algebra to FP64. This repairs diagnostic cancellation rather than policy precision or trainer behavior. The very smallest positive values still lack independent high-precision reference certification.

Finite KL agrees with the local Fisher quadratic within 1.55% at all six snapshots and within .0452% at 8M. Full logit-chord linearization errors range .002389–.023437; JVP component additivity errors range 9.96e-8–1.40e-7. Grams are approximately symmetric, maximum absolute asymmetry 1.16e-10, and symmetric-part eigenvalues are positive in all six. These are useful internal numerical contracts. Fisher and JVP computations remain FP32; the repaired KL is FP64 over FP32 shapes. JVP additivity alone cannot independently certify the derivative implementation.

Cross-layer terms strongly reinforce total motion: joint Q is 3.34–4.53 times the diagonal sum. Ranking isolated layer costs cannot explain deployed policy displacement. Largest isolated diagonal contributors at 4M are first-block hidden matrices. At 8M, head weight has the largest diagonal in rollout half and v4 half, while first-block up weight remains largest in rollout full. Thus relative component geometry changes materially even though the total proposal shrinks: 4M/8M joint-Q ratios are 16198, 16839 and 28359 respectively. This cannot be interpreted as a causal loss of expressivity or efficiency: the saved optimizer scale/schedule, policy, states, gradients and momentum history all change between snapshots.
