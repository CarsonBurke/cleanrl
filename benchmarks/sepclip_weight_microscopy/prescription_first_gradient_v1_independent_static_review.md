## Independent comparison and mathematical scope

The actor-only functional graph uses actual actor logits, the actual 1+softplus Beta shape law, native actions and the same action-scale log-density convention, exact recovered first permutation, saved advantages and source advantage normalization. Its negative maximum of the two loss branches preserves the source clamp/maximum tie derivatives. A hard Boolean saturation mask with a different equality convention would not necessarily do so.

Entropy coefficient must be zero, and actor/critic parameters must be identity-disjoint and cover the full agent. Under these conditions the actor part of the source raw loss gradient is the negative policy-ascent covector; critic value loss cannot contribute to actor parameters. Separate clipping applies a common nonnegative scalar to that actor loss covector. This establishes the mathematical expected relation in real arithmetic. It does not guarantee bitwise equality of the independent actor-only graph, because its differentiation, fusion and reduction path can differ from the actual full source loss graph.

For ascent u and negative recorded clipped-loss covector v, the reported common scale is (u dot v)/(u dot u). The residual is ||v-scale*u||, and the cosine is (u dot v)/(||u||*||v||). These formulas correctly compare the full concatenated actor covector, not merely selected directional pairings. The scale is unconstrained, and an opposite covector correctly yields a negative scale flag. Null u produces an undefined scale/cosine rather than an invented multiplier. A null v has an undefined relative residual. Parameter and route summaries accompany the full actor result; residuals using the single full-actor scale prevent independently refitted route scales from concealing incompatible clipping behavior.

No arbitrary residual tolerance converts the independent comparison into an equivalence certificate. The accepted status refers to exact-source first clipped-gradient reconstruction, not independently certified equivalence of the second graph. Raw source gradients/preclip norms and LS scales remain reconstructed or fitted diagnostics. Even a positive common scale and tiny residual do not turn them into independently recorded clipping factors or physical policy gradients. This is a stored-surrogate/GAE/action measurement, not a causal return derivative.

## Coverage and acceptance boundary

The 23 unexecuted cases comprise four independent actual-source recording fixtures (v14 additive/original, v15 original and advantage normalization enabled), twelve corruptions, a source permutation/RNG contract, three malformed permutation contracts, an independent exact-vector scale/residual witness, null/opposite covectors, and separate actor/critic clipping arithmetic. Corruptions include actor and critic gradient edits, missing coverage, dtype/layout changes, NaN, entropy, shuffle state, observation row ordering, advantage conventions/values and missing saved shuffle state. RNG preservation is checked after exceptions.

A nonblocking coverage limitation remains: the actual-PPO fixtures record old-policy logprobs, so ratios are near one. They do not directly exercise mixed-sign advantages in saturated branches or exact clip-boundary ties. The reviewed implementation preserves the correct source expression, but those boundary behaviors have no dedicated new numerical witness in this suite. A corrupted permutation may change a full-batch reduction only through floating-point ordering; the static review does not guarantee the wrong-shuffle fixture's sensitivity on every compiler/runtime.

No blocking source or mathematical defect was found. Numerical acceptance requires the queued CUDA contracts and complete bitwise first-gradient parity on every actual capture, with pinned source/checkpoint/trace/training manifest/execution identity. Report independent full-covector norms, cosine, common scale and residuals as measured values without assuming a favorable outcome. Failure withholds the reconstruction and downstream comparison; it must not be repaired by arbitrary tolerance changes. The bridge does not establish optimizer continuation, unclipped-gradient historical truth, full physical-PG reliability, retained-range efficiency, near-optimality or benchmark-score causation.
