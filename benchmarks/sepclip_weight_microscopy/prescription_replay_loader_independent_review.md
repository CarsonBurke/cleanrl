# Prescription replay loader: independent static review

Approved source preparation for queued numerical validation. Reviewed `scripts/sepclip_prescription_replay.py` SHA256 7edfc432775a0098a60d63cb3c8642c65a599c3e8ae8fa2cc2f3354885d1dcf9 and `tests/test_sepclip_prescription_replay.py` SHA256 b34993d92500366a9d58a02d58d67ad0edcec937b692370061e2a1820d67e44d. No numerical tests, models or jobs were executed or submitted by this reviewer, and no frozen trainer was edited. Thirty-five statically expanded contract cases are prepared; this is not an executed-case count or passing result.

The loader allows only exact v14/v15 modules, verifies immutable source/execution/checkpoint/trace byte pins before deserialization, binds execution command/module/experiment name and derives runtime Args as the frozen trainer does. Imported source location and source hashes are checked. It repeats source/artifact pin checks after reconstruction. Exact model keys, parameter shapes/dtypes and fixed physical bounds/scale buffers are required. Batch FP32 policy records and per-row normalization capture have explicit shape/finite checks; preclip normalization overflow is rejected rather than hidden by clipping. Compiled old forward checks latent finiteness and separately compares actual alpha/beta/value/logprob against independently recorded fields.

The absence of independently recorded old logits is explicitly reported, including shape-one counts and the absence of inversion or floor-latent certification. Forward consistency does not recover missing latent information. Recorded native actions are checked against clipping bounds and mapped to saved physical actions within the declared FP32 tolerance. Capture semantics require pre-update, per-env normalization and the recorded complete integration backend fields.

Trace reconstruction checks full actor AND critic parameter coverage for clipped gradients, ambient deltas and retracted deltas, update/epoch order, complete fixed-epoch count, step agreement and finite reconstructed states. v15 entries must name the actual geometry and matching ambient/intrinsic delta stage. Only architecture-specific constrained axes are asserted unit: additive v14 free trunk matrices/projection/amplitudes are not projected; original v14 and v15 body gate/up/down axes are 1,1,0, and both heads use unit rows. No retraction is added during replay. Endpoint actor banks come directly from the recorded full endpoint; first bank is old plus the saved first FP32 delta and is expressly not an independently recorded intermediate. Accumulated endpoint reconstruction uses declared 2e-5 tolerance and exposes its error. Immutable buffers must not change.

Optional warm optimizer loading constructs the exact chosen module's factory. It requires matching stacked state coverage/schema/dtypes, finite rails and nonnegative second moments, and actual optimizer method/precision/slow-clock metadata. v15 native loading rejects geometry/axis reinterpretation; additional checks require state_version=2 and stack-specific axes. Intrinsic base points and diagnostics must be finite, the base point unit within 1e-4, sticky pathology flag zero, momentum tangent within the declared absolute tolerance and base-to-current geodesic denominator positive. Raw and renormalized basepoint/current residuals are reported, without claiming a calibrated proximity bound or bitwise equality. This preserves the legitimate FP32 post-normalization difference documented in the frozen update. Adam's non-LR group semantics, finite recorded FP32 state and nonnegative second moment are checked separately. Nothing is loaded through v7 and no optimizer step is used to create replay banks.

The contracts independently record policy/value/likelihood through actual public model APIs, reject incompatible module/provenance/Args/capture/trace/state metadata, preserve nonunit v14 projection weights, and test exact saved endpoint/first-bank identities. Compiled head-bias JVP has an independent affine derivative reference. The additive projection radial JVP is nonzero and agrees with a separate compiled centered primal difference; a legacy row-tangent projection erases that response. This is a useful detector of the specific architecture-assumption error.

Coverage limits remain material. The warm-state fixture contains initial zero matrix rails and no populated Adam history: it exercises loader schema/domain checks, not independently executed nonzero warm continuation. The saved first-chord fixture moves head bias only, while the nonlinear projection test uses a constructed direction. These do not replace a separate compiled-primal finite-difference contract on the complete first/end chords of actual trial captures. Integration fixtures are synthetic and do not independently validate physical state restoration. The absolute reconstruction/unit/tangency tolerances require telemetry review on actual captures. A large finite basepoint/current residual is reported rather than rejected by an invented capture bound; source approval does not establish its historical correctness.

The separately approved new closed-loop driver uses this loader's complete actual banks and original fixed critic. Any physical interpretation still requires executed numerical contracts, actual trial artifacts and scope-appropriate conditional uncertainty. This loader review provides neither independent PG reliability, an efficiency estimate, warm optimizer causal superiority nor a benchmark-score explanation.
