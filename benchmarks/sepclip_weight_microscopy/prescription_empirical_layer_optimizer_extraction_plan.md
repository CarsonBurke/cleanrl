Gate/up/head layer routes combine weight AND bias, whereas a matrix bank contains weights only. Consequently a layer Fisher/covector credit cannot automatically be assigned to its matrix optimizer stage; its bias may contribute or compensate through Adam. Mixing and readout gain remain separate routes. This is an explicit attribution limit rather than an invitation to infer the missing split.

For each bank member and epoch use exported norms/dots/cosines to quantify the actually reconstructed chain G→blend B→lookahead N→cascade C→lane-adapted A→norm-restored E→final applied Q→master displacement. Source directions C/E/Q are LOSS descent directions before the explicit negative update sign. Directly available G·B, G·N and G·C identify whether momentum/lookahead or cascade altered local recorded-gradient alignment. Compare norm ratios separately from angles; a smaller dot need not mean worse alignment when the norm changed. Undefined cosine/null directions stay undefined. Singular values of C characterize the actual cascade response, not an assumed exact polar factor or an optimizer optimum.

Existing outputs give cos(C,A), cos(C,E), ||C||, ||A||, ||E||, ||Q||, quantization error and actual displacement credit, but NOT direct G·A/G·E/G·Q. Do not turn C-to-E cosine into G-to-E credit. Two conservative analyses are available without changing frozen diagnostics:

- A geometry-only interval: let gpar=(G·C)/||C||, gperp=sqrt(||G||²-gpar²), and c=cos(C,E). Then G·E lies within ||E||*(c*gpar ± sqrt(1-c²)*gperp). This uses the actual exported vector relationships; substantial negative C-to-E rotation does not force a specific credit sign. Invalid/roundoff-inconsistent cosine/norm inputs withhold the interval rather than introduce an unreported floor.
- A displacement-derived conditional interval: lambda=source_lr*aspect_scale>0 and p=(-G·ambient_delta)/lambda estimate G·Q. Its error includes ||G||*(master-subtraction-rounding_norm + whole-bank independent-stage-delta-error_norm)/lambda. To compare with E add ||G||*||Q-E||. The exported source proposal is itself FP32(-lambda*Q), so a strict real-valued pairing additionally needs a declared scaling-multiplication roundoff allowance, or the result must be labelled an engineering proxy. No omitted multiplication error is silently declared zero. The whole-bank delta error is a conservative member bound. At small LR these intervals can become unresolved.

Use intervals only to state changes whose sign/magnitude remains resolved. If G·C is reliably positive but the equalized-credit interval is strictly negative, the lane/equalization stage has changed local recorded-gradient credit sign under the stated accepted stage/rounding scope. If intervals overlap zero or the before-stage value, report amplitude/rotation only and leave credit attribution unresolved. An exact G·adapted value cannot be recovered from these summaries. No new alternative optimizer proposal or extra diagnostic is needed to report these existing bounds; a future version would be required if exact missing pairings become material.
