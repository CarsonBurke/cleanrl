# Acceptance gates for pending PG, geometry and chart artifacts

This is an artifact-review checklist, not an executed result. It supplements existing source reviews and preserves their receipts. No models/tests/jobs were run or submitted to prepare it. An exit code alone does not authorize efficiency, near-optimum or chart-selection interpretation.

| Job | Expected artifact and scope | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11765 | dense_pg_controls.json, four matched v12 control snapshots; `results` | Defaults are 32 selected states, 16 first-action samples, TWO independent replicas, horizons 1/16/64/256/512, gamma .99. Confirm executed protocol rather than assume defaults. |
| 11781 | oracle_geometry_historical.json; historical physics_complete.json input, horizons 64/256 | This is not the new dense H512 oracle. Inspect state_distribution_matched and per-case/replica state layout before comparisons. |
| 11782 | actual_step_dense.json; consumes 11765, horizons 256/512 | First saved update and all-epoch endpoint chords linearized at the old snapshot; these are not all-time physical PG or sequential ten-epoch local derivatives. |
| 11775 | chart_numerics.json | Synthetic numerical grid and actual candidate decode-range stress test, not visited-state performance or chart choice. |

1. **Provenance and completion.** Require execution manifest exit 0, explicit maxParallelRuns1, expected source hashes, artifact byte hash and complete expected snapshot/horizon/case inventory. Reconcile source repairs against executed hashes without overwriting earlier receipts. Require applicable numerical contracts (dense11764, geometry/rank11776, chart11774 and relevant actual-chord contract) to pass on matching executed source. Verify no artifact is a partial incrementally written file; geometry writes results as each case completes. Match snapshot SHA, oracle SHA, state order, normalized observations, old policy/logit replay and trace identity before pairing. Missing cases, mismatched provenance or any unexpected NaN/Infinity: reject interpretation and retain failure evidence.

2. **Physical fork validity.** For 11765 require dense_boundary_replay within source thresholds (GAE/bootstrap2e-5, applied reward1e-6), physical restore and saved first-transition errors within1e-10, and analytic-score replay telemetry consistent with the existing contract. Reject resets/terminations or preclip/accumulation nonfinite values. Replicas must share selected states/metric/objective, have declared dimensions and mean equal replica mean. Observed-action Q replicas reduce continuation noise only; policy_logit_pg includes independently resampled first actions and continuation noise. Do not merge those uncertainty scopes.

3. **Sampler and objective scope.** Retain first_action_only=true, fixed continuation policy, frozen per-selected-state observation/reward normalization, finite discounted horizon and separate estimated old-critic tail. The first-action law uses uniforms in [1e-7,1-1e-7], clips native values to [1e-6,1-1e-6] then casts FP32; continuous Beta scores omit boundary-atom/quantile-truncation/quantization corrections. Record actual atom counts and theoretical tail mass. Small mass is not a bias bound: absent derivative-weighted correction/bound, describe PG as this score estimator under the diagnostic sampling law. Raw, frozen normalized and bootstrap-normalized fields are different objectives. Require paired raw/normalized/tail fixed-motion gains before attributing sign changes to optimizer geometry. Dense clipped FP64 reward accumulation is not silently equivalent to closed-loop per-reward FP32 rounding or online training normalizers.

4. **Fisher and chart algebra.** Require finite positive Beta shapes, finite symmetric full 2x2 Fisher blocks and positive eigenvalues before inverse/root/solve; examine smallest eigenvalue and conditioning, not SPD alone. Match root/inverse-root pairing and the same selected-state weighting. Check joint energy against the SUM of every Gram entry, including cross-layer and cross-coordinate terms. Require finite retracted KL computed in FP64 on identical actual rounded FP32 old/new shapes; retain signs, absolute error and KL-to-local-Q discrepancy rather than repair by clipping negative KL. The existing geometry Fisher recomputes softplus in FP64 from FP32 logits: label that smooth numerical metric, distinct from actual rounded-shape KL. Native mean/total-concentration coordinates are not the v13 actual-excess r/ell chart. Shape-one boundaries cannot be clamped into an interior chart, and nonfinite/singular geometry must be withheld per state with counts and the original selected-state denominator preserved.

5. **Rank and finite response.** Review all threshold_sensitivity entries: configured arithmetic threshold and relative cutoffs1e-7,1e-6,1e-5,1e-4. Report rank, retained condition number, reachable dual fraction and required parameter norm together. FP64 projection/SVD of FP32 derivatives does not supply FP64 Jacobian accuracy. If apparent near-full rank, reachable fraction or efficiency depends on the tiniest modes, withhold a robust capacity/near-optimum claim. Require independent compiled-primal chord evidence, correct ambient versus tangent path, finite-retraction error and actual finite KL alongside target/achieved local Q. A fitted minimum-norm interpolation with large coefficients or failed finite response is not a valid causal training prescription. Legacy unit-matrix geometry applies only to its original actor sources; new additive v14 requires the architecture-specific loader/driver.

6. **Reliability before efficiency.** For 11782 inspect cross_replica_signal_noise and preserve signed cross energies, noise estimates and unresolved flags. TWO replicas give one pair energy; a positive value alone does not resolve the mean physical field, while a negative value is not proof of zero population signal. Pair entries sharing replicas are not independent samples. Do not positivity-clip signed signal or substitute its square root into an efficiency denominator. For fixed saved motions, inspect every signed replica gain and approximate Student-t conditional interval (R2 critical12.706), not only the mean/cosine. A directional sign or superiority claim is withheld if unresolved, even when geometry is numerically correct. A resolved scalar direction can be reported without claiming the entire field is resolved.

7. **Fixed, fitted and retained-range comparisons.** An actual saved update is fixed conditional on the snapshot/training history and independent oracle draws; a fitted direction must use separate replica evaluation. Inspect fit versus held-replica gains, finite held-replica gains and finite retraction/KL. Reciprocal fit/test pairs share data and are not independent validation repetitions. Choosing a winner using held-replica results consumes that holdout. Both field AND actual motion must be projected into the SAME retained range for a retained-range Cauchy ratio; separately report discarded field/motion energy. An observed noisy-field ratio can be accepted as algebra, but physical optimizer efficiency or near-optimum requires resolved field/credit uncertainty, sampler/objective qualification and stable numerical range. Geometry alone is never that gate.

8. **Chart numerical readiness.** Inspect every visited-grid density, entropy, logit/chart score error and FP32/FP64-same-shape KL test; near-zero relative KL errors need absolute errors and sign. Inspect actual_candidate_decode_range including known nonfinite stress cases/null reporting, backward finiteness and shape-one rounding. Sampler moments reference unclipped Beta, so assess clipping mass/counts and host-versus-CUDA shape parity separately. A passing contract that intentionally detects extreme-range failure does not prove that training remains inside a safe range. Withhold candidate selection until actual trial concentration/shape occupancy, actual r/ell physical covectors with cross metric, architecture-specific finite effects and relevant causal benchmark evidence are available. No synthetic grid row selects a training concentration.

Practical disposition: accept valid provenance/numerical contracts as measurements; accept resolved conditional signed credit only within its stated sampling, state and objective scope; label unresolved geometry/field statistics descriptive. Withhold retained-range physical efficiency, global near-optimum, chart selection and causal score explanations whenever their separate gates remain unmet. Preserve failures and uncertainty rather than converting them into favorable denominators or candidate choices.
