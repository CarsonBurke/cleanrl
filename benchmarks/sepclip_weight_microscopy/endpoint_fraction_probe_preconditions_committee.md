# Conditional decision on an endpoint-fraction physical diagnostic

This is a prospective read-only committee judgment, not a new result or submission. The active synthesis and endpoint_fraction_feedback_identification_contract.md correctly separate first-action credit, full feedback and sampled/deterministic objectives. The old v12 Anvil8M approximately3,000-fold gap is a reason to investigate response scaling, not proof that curvature caused it. Layer/moment/first-gradient results are still pending in the current synthesis.

## Eligibility after those results

- Reconcile the actual source, old/endpoint banks, complete ten-update trace and full nonlinear chord-JVP gate for the SPECIFIC capture being probed. If new gradient/optimizer work reveals a loading, bank, clipping or trace reconstruction error affecting that capture, resolve the error before measuring fractions. Accepted v14 layer/optimizer reconstruction does not independently certify the different old v12 capture.
- Keep an exact old→saved-endpoint chord, with exact endpoint banks at fractions0 and1, all actor parameters, and an explicitly pinned interior arithmetic/rounding law. First-update weights reconstructed from a saved delta are a different bank, not a fraction of ten-update time. No projection/retraction or alternate actor may silently change the path.
- Lock the target to the same selected old v12 states, own frozen normalizer rows, H512, and one declared reward/sampling convention if investigating its existing gap. A v14 or NorMuon fraction result cannot adjudicate that gap through different own occupancies. A normalized score comparison must reconcile FP64 versus clip-then-FP32 reward arithmetic and retain continuous-score versus rounded/clipped/truncated-generator limits.
- Specify the inferential question before physical returns: finite t0-response nonlinearity, near-old slope sign, or full feedback coupling. Declare fractions, primary contrast/objective, secondary analyses, family and uncertainty rule before seeing gains. Choose useful fractions from represented parameter/action motion and numerical contracts, not from whichever physical signs look best. No currently registered rule supplies a universal near-old precision threshold or fraction choice.
- Pair all required fractions/arms on identical per-state quantiles within each replica. If using the old fraction1 return, certify that its complete draw layout and stream are identical; reuse of the same integer seed alone is insufficient. Otherwise fraction1 must belong to the same newly specified panel as the fractions it is contrasted with. Pin independent replica streams and any cross-panel relationship. Current dense and closed-loop producers reuse the same seed formula with different draw layouts; cross-panel independence and ratio uncertainty are not certified.
- Keep restore/saved-transition/normalization/bounds/finite/no-reset gates, actual-source future actors and full replica/state arrays. Moment or stored-loss-gradient alignment may motivate interpretation but cannot substitute for physical credit or prove that all-time feedback is helpful.

## Decision criteria without invented thresholds

Let T(f) be the first-action-only fraction gain, L(f) the later-only gain, F(f) the full gain, and I(f)=F(f)−T(f)−L(f), all paired against old/old. This exact finite identity retains covariance and is not a critic or contact decomposition.

A resolved paired T(f) establishes only the finite conditional sign at that fraction. To address response scaling, form D_T(f)=T(f)−2*T(f/2) inside each replica and state. A resolved nonzero D_T establishes departure from linear scaling over those measured intervals. It does not identify the limiting derivative or distinguish physical contact changes, distribution/actor nonlinearity and numerical-law effects. The analogous D_F concerns full policy response and must not be compared with first-action PG.

A near-old slope claim requires multiple useful T(f)/f secants, a predeclared accuracy target, resolved uncertainty on their changes, and an effective-motion/roundoff plateau. Failure to reject a change is not evidence of equivalence. Contact switching and rounded/clipped actions can obstruct a smooth derivative interpretation. No such plateau or target is available from current endpoint summaries.

A resolved I(f) establishes finite first/later feedback interaction under the declared law. Its sign does not assign a mechanism to contacts, particular layers, critic error or optimizer moments. Positive full F with negative T can arise through L and I; unresolved L/I cannot identify a unique cause even if F has a pointwise sign.

With four replicas, construct every primary/secondary contrast within each replica, average over the32 fixed states, and estimate continuation uncertainty across the four replica averages. Use the declared family rule. The existing t3 pointwise intervals and two-replica scalar precision rule are not a new preregistration, nor a state/seed/generalization certificate. No ratio of noisy PG and physical means is an inferential test without their covariance/bias treatment.

## Completion outcomes

If fractions remain statistically or numerically unresolved, close this line as inconclusive rather than choose another fraction after inspecting signs. If finite nonlinearity resolves, report that narrow conditional phenomenon with action-law and occupancy limits; do not invent a contact cause. If a near-old secant is supported but disagrees with smooth-score PG, classify the mismatch as unresolved estimator/objective/law disagreement until those components are independently reconciled. Agreement of signs alone does not validate scale. If the accepted layer/moment/gradient evidence already resolves the relevant implementation defect or excludes a causal proposal, a new fraction measurement need not be run merely to complete the queue.

The present evidence therefore supports considering a bounded, preregistered paired diagnostic of the actual old endpoint after source/gradient/trace adjudication. It does not compel one, select its fractions, authorize execution, suggest a training change or establish a global explanation of sub10k versus20k. A complete investigation may honestly end with a measured feedback interaction and unresolved local-score magnitude.
